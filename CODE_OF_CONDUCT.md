# Community conduct

Open CI Hub welcomes participants regardless of background or experience. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate in issues, discussions, reviews and code. No harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, doxxing, or posting credentials. Discuss disagreements about code and safety with evidence, not personal attacks. Maintainers may remove abusive contributions and restrict participation when necessary.

For sensitive security issues, follow `docs/SECURITY.md` rather than opening a public issue.
